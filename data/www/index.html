Desk Buddy

Shortcut syntax

Keys (over Bluetooth) — modifiers cmd ctrl alt shift joined with +: cmd+space, cmd+shift+4, ctrl+cmd+q. Keys: letters, digits, enter esc tab space backspace delete up down left right home end pageup pagedown f1…f24.

Media keys — playpause next prev volup voldown mute stop.

Sequences — separate steps with ;   wait:300 pauses, text:hello types text. spot:Notes opens an app through Spotlight.

Mac action — the id of an entry in the helper's actions.json (open an app, run a Shortcuts automation, run a script). It needs the Mac helper running.

Labels are up to 6 characters. Up to 6 pages with 6 shortcuts each. The whole layout must stay under 3800 characters.

Import / export JSON

Preview of the knob screen. Turn the knob to change page.

Timer presets

On the buddy: swipe to the timer screen, turn the knob to pick a preset, press to start.

Google Calendar

Paste the web-app URL from your Apps Script deployment (it ends in /exec?t=…). See google/calendar_proxy.gs.

    Mac helper

    A tiny script on your Mac that opens apps and runs automations when you press a Mac action button, and sends CPU load to the buddy.

    Access token

    Goes into the helper's config.json. Keep it private.

    Clock face

    Accent colour

    Photo for the Photo face
    
      
    One photo, with the time on top. Choose it, drag to move it and zoom until it fills the round screen. It is cropped and shrunk to 240×240 here in your browser before it is sent, so the buddy keeps only a small file. Re-uploading the web pages with pio run -t uploadfs erases it.

    Weather
    
      
    Shows the temperature and conditions above the time on the clock screen. The data comes from Open-Meteo, which is free and needs no account. Only the coordinates of the city you choose are sent.

      WiFi networks
      
      
      The buddy remembers up to 5 networks (home, office, phone hotspot…). It joins the first one in this list that is in range, and if it loses WiFi it moves on to the next. Only 2.4 GHz networks work. Passwords are kept on the buddy and are never shown again.
